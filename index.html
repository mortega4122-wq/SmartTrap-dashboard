Replace weekly trap checks with counts that report in on their own.

Most orchards still monitor pests by checking traps by hand, often once a week. Between visits, pest activity goes unseen.

SmartTrap closes that gap. Every trap counts insects as they arrive and sends the data, along with temperature and humidity, to one dashboard.

Counts insects automatically

An infrared sensor registers each insect that enters the trap. No manual counting.

The whole orchard in one view

Every trap on a satellite map, with its latest counts, conditions, and battery level.

Built to be affordable

No cameras and no image processing. Low-power traps share one gateway to keep the cost per trap down.
