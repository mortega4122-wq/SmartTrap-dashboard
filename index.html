How it works

  1. Detect

    Insects entering the trap break an infrared beam, and each detection is logged with a timestamp.

  2. Record

    The trap also measures temperature and humidity and keeps a local backup, so no data is lost.

  3. Relay

    A field gateway collects data from nearby traps and sends it to the cloud over cellular.

  4. Act

    Your dashboard shows trap maps, history charts, and heatmaps across the orchard.
